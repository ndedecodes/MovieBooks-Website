A List of my Favourite Books

  1. Atomic Habits by James Clear

    Atomic Habits by James Clear is a self-development book that explores how small, consistent changes can lead to remarkable results over time. It teaches the importance of building good habits, breaking bad ones, and making simple improvements every day. I love this book because it reminds me that personal growth does not happen overnight but through small steps taken consistently.

    Learn more about Atomic Habits

  2. The Alchemist by Paul Coelho

    The Alchemist by Paulo Coelho is a philosophical novel that follows the journey of Santiago, a young shepherd who travels from Spain to Egypt in search of a hidden treasure. Along the way, he discovers valuable lessons about following his dreams, listening to his heart, and trusting the journey of life. I love this book because it reminds me to believe in my dreams and embrace the lessons that come with pursuing them.

    "There is only one thing that makes a dream impossible to achieve: the fear of failure"
    Learn more about The Alchemist

  3. Little Women Louisa M.Alcot

    Little Women by Louisa May Alcott is a timeless classic that follows the lives of the four March sisters—Meg, Jo, Beth, and Amy—as they grow from childhood into womanhood in nineteenth-century New England. Through their shared struggles, creative pursuits, family bonds, and individual ambitions, the novel explores the true meaning of love, independence, and character. I love this book because Jo March's fierce determination and passion for writing are deeply inspiring, reminding me to stay true to myself and pursue my own path with courage.

    “I’m not ambitious for a splendid fortune, but I know, by experience, how much genuine happiness can be had in a plain little house, where the daily bead is earned, and some privations give sweetness to the few pleasures. I am content to see Meg begin humbly, for if I am not mistaken, she will be rich in the possession of a good man’s heart, and that is better than a fortune.”
    Learn more about Little Women

My Favourite Book⭐

Think Big by Dr. Ben Carson


Think Big by Dr. Ben Carson is one of my favourite books because it inspires me to believe in my abilities, think beyond my limitations, and pursue excellence. I love how it teaches the importance of determination, hard work, education, and having a positive mindset. It reminds me that my background does not determine how far I can go in life.

There are loyal hearts, there are spirits brave, There are souls that are pure and true; Then give the world the best you have, And the best will come back to you.

A List of my Favourite Movies

  1. The Devil Wears Prada

    The Devil Wears Prada is one of my favourite fashion-themed movies. It follows Andy Sachs, a young aspiring journalist who lands a job as an assistant to Miranda Priestly, the powerful and demanding editor of a prestigious fashion magazine. I love this movie because of its glamorous fashion, unforgettable characters, witty humour, and lessons about ambition, confidence, and staying true to yourself.

    Click here to learn more about The Devil Wears Prada.

  2. Ratatouille

    Ratatouille is a heartwarming animated movie about Remy, a little rat with an extraordinary passion for cooking. Despite being a rat, Remy dreams of becoming a great chef and finds an unexpected opportunity to pursue his dream in a Paris restaurant. I love this movie because of its beautiful animation, humour, creativity, and inspiring message that anyone can achieve greatness regardless of where they come from. Its famous message, "Anyone can cook," reminds me to believe in my dreams and never give up.

    Click here to learn more about Ratatouillie.

  3. Legally Blonde

    Legally Blonde is a fun and inspiring comedy film about Elle Woods, a fashionable and determined young woman who enrolls at Harvard Law School to prove that she is more than just her appearance. Along the way, she challenges stereotypes, discovers her potential, and proves that intelligence, confidence, and hard work can take you far.

    Click here to learn more about Legally Blonde.
